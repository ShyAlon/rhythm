Rhythm privacy policy

Rhythm is a small personal habit tracker. This page says exactly what it stores, where, and why.

What is stored

  • Account: your email address. Passwords are never stored in plain text - they are salted bcrypt hashes handled by Supabase Auth. There are no usernames.
  • Your data: habit, bonus, and penalty names, emojis, notes, schedules, score weights, and your day-by-day completion history.
  • Push notifications: if you enable reminders, each device stores a Web Push endpoint and browser-generated encryption keys. These are not passwords; they are how notifications reach your device.

Where it lives

  • On your device, in local storage - the app works fully offline.
  • In a Supabase Postgres database (EU region) if you create an account, for cross-device sync. Row-level security means your rows are readable and writable only by your account.

What leaves the building

  • Push reminders travel through your browser vendor's push service (Apple, Google, or Mozilla) to reach your device. The notification text includes the habit name you chose.
  • Nothing is sold, shared, or used for analytics or ads. There are no trackers in the app.

Deletion

  • Account tab - Delete account wipes your account and every row of your cloud data, immediately and permanently. The local copy on your device remains under your control.
  • You can export everything as JSON from the app at any time.
